The Rosary brings prayer and reflection together as we contemplate the life of Jesus with Mary. You may pray alone, with your family, or with a group.

## Begin

1. Make the Sign of the Cross and recite the Apostles’ Creed while holding the crucifix.
2. On the first large bead, pray an Our Father.
3. Pray a Hail Mary on each of the three small beads, then the Glory Be.

## Pray five decades

A decade consists of ten Hail Marys, accompanied by meditation on a mystery.

1. Name the first mystery and pause to reflect on it.
2. Pray an Our Father on the large bead.
3. Pray ten Hail Marys on the small beads, keeping the mystery in mind.
4. Finish the decade with the Glory Be. The Fatima prayer may follow.
5. Continue with the remaining four mysteries, praying each decade in the same way.

## Conclude

After five decades, pray the Hail, Holy Queen and the concluding Rosary prayer. Finish with the Sign of the Cross.

For the full prayer texts, see [How to Pray the Rosary — USCCB](https://www.usccb.org/how-to-pray-the-rosary).

## Choosing the mysteries

This is a customary weekly pattern. The mysteries may be adapted for the season or the community’s prayer.

| Mysteries | Usual days |
| :--- | :--- |
| Joyful | Monday and Saturday |
| Luminous | Thursday |
| Sorrowful | Tuesday and Friday |
| Glorious | Wednesday and Sunday |

### Joyful Mysteries

1. The Annunciation
2. The Visitation
3. The Nativity
4. The Presentation in the Temple
5. The Finding of Jesus in the Temple

### Luminous Mysteries

1. The Baptism of Jesus in the Jordan
2. The Wedding at Cana
3. The Proclamation of the Kingdom
4. The Transfiguration
5. The Institution of the Eucharist

### Sorrowful Mysteries

1. The Agony in the Garden
2. The Scourging at the Pillar
3. The Crowning with Thorns
4. The Carrying of the Cross
5. The Crucifixion

### Glorious Mysteries

1. The Resurrection
2. The Ascension
3. The Descent of the Holy Spirit
4. The Assumption of Mary
5. The Coronation of Mary

The [Holy See’s Rosary guide](https://www.vatican.va/special/rosary/documents/misteri_en.html) explains the mysteries and their customary days. The [Rosary Center’s guide](https://www.rosarycenter.org/how-to-pray-the-rosary/) also provides a sequence and prayer resources.
