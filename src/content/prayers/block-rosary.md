The Block Rosary brings neighbors and families together to contemplate the life of Jesus with Mary. The image of the Blessed Virgin remains in each home for one week, with a member of the household leading the prayer.

## Praying together

The leader reads **℣.** and everyone responds **℟.** Unless otherwise indicated, prayers without these marks are recited by all. Instructions appear in red and are not read aloud. Bible verse references need not be read aloud.

## The order of prayer

1. When the image arrives, a member of the host family prays the **Acceptance Prayer**. Skip this prayer on other evenings.
2. Pray **Mary Comes to Our Home**, sing **Immaculate Mother**, and continue with **Welcome to Mary** and **And Mary Said**.
3. Kneel, as circumstances permit, and pray the opening verses, the Creed, an Our Father, three Hail Marys, and the Glory Be.
4. Pray five mysteries. For each one, read the meditation and spiritual fruit, then pray an Our Father, ten Hail Marys, the Glory Be, and the Fatima Prayer. The Invocation of the Miraculous Medal may follow.
5. Pray the **Hail, Holy Queen**, the **Rosary Collect**, and the **Litany of the Blessed Virgin Mary**.
6. Stand for the **Family Prayer**, then sing **Give Thanks**.
7. When the image is leaving for the next home, pray the **Farewell Prayer**. On other evenings, skip it. Conclude with the Sign of the Cross.

## Choosing the mysteries

The guide suggests the mysteries for the day. You may choose another set for your community’s prayer. Customarily, the Joyful Mysteries may be prayed on Sundays during Advent and Christmas, and the Sorrowful Mysteries on Sundays during Lent.
