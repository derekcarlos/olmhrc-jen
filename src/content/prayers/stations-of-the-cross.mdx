Follow Jesus from the Last Supper to the joy of the Resurrection. Pray alone, with your family, or with our chapel community. There is no need to leave the guide to look up the Our Father, Hail Mary, or Glory Be: their complete texts are included at each station.

## How to pray

Begin with the Sign of the Cross and the opening prayer. At each station, pray the adoration and response, read the passage, pause for reflection, and pray the station prayer. End each station with one Our Father, one Hail Mary, and one Glory Be.

After the fourteenth station, pray another Our Father, Hail Mary, and Glory Be for the intention of the Holy Father, followed by the final prayer, concluding blessing, and the Sign of the Cross.

The leader reads **℣.** and everyone responds **℟.** Prayers without a leader/response mark may be said by everyone. Red italic text gives instructions and is not read aloud.

An asterisk (\*) marks a pause for breath when praying aloud together. Paragraph breaks are preserved. Songs are not included in this guide.

## About this version

The readings and station prayers follow **[The New Way of the Cross](https://www.god-answers-prayers.com/seasonal_novena_prayer/new_way_of_the_cross/index.html)** from God Answers Prayers, using the text supplied for this guide. Songs are omitted; the Our Father, Hail Mary, and Glory Be are included at the end of every station.

The Our Father, Hail Mary, and Glory Be are shared with our [English Rosary Guide](/prayers/how-to-pray-the-holy-rosary#prayer-guide).
