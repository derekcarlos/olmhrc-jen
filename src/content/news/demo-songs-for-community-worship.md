Music updates could help singers and neighbors prepare for a chapel celebration. An article can introduce the people coordinating the music and point readers to useful resources.

## A helpful music update

- Identify the celebration and its confirmed date.
- Share the agreed rehearsal arrangements.
- Link to the Songs page for resources approved for that celebration.

This example does not announce a rehearsal or a confirmed selection of songs. It demonstrates a possible music update.
