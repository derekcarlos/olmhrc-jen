> **Preview content:** This is a sample article for testing the News layout and pagination. It is not a confirmed chapel announcement.

News posts can introduce practical ways for neighbors to support the chapel and explain how to get involved.

## What a volunteer announcement should explain

- The specific help needed.
- The expected time commitment and any preparation.
- Who to contact and how to express interest.

These are illustrative ideas, rather than an open volunteer registration. Add confirmed opportunities and a contact person before publishing a real invitation.
