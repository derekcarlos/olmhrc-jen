Join our community for Simbang Gabi (Misa de Aguinaldo), celebrated each evening from **December 15 to 23 at 7:30 PM**.

During **Simbang Gabi**, Sunday Mass is celebrated at **7:30 PM only**. There is no 3:30 PM Mass on Sundays within these dates.

For any schedule changes, please check [News](/news).
