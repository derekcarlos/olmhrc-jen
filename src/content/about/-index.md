Our Lady of the Most Holy Rosary Chapel is located in Jubilation Enclave North, Biñan, Laguna.

Rosarium is our small online home for neighbors to stay connected in faith through chapel news, Mass information, Scripture readings, prayers, and songs.

Centered on our devotion to Our Lady of the Most Holy Rosary, may this space help us know one another, support our chapel, and make faith part of our daily lives.
